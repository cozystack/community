## Agenda

Every meeting follows the same shape:

1. **Introduction** — new attendees are invited to introduce themselves.
2. **Agenda and notes** — topics submitted ahead of time.
3. **Open floor** — anything an attendee wants to raise.
4. **Maintainer topics** — community issues, upcoming releases, the good first
issue list.
5. **Pull requests that need attention** and a bug scrub.

## Adding a topic

Add your item to the *Agenda and Notes* section for the upcoming date in the [shared document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1YObSKsQ3Gzi1gSLPlUp-PbSYEeVkoyzY20M_hXKVhq4/edit) before the meeting starts. Prefix your comments with `[name]` so the notes stay readable afterwards.

To get edit access to the document, subscribe to the [cncf-cozystack-dev mailing list](https://lists.cncf.io/g/cncf-cozystack-dev) — members of the list are granted edit rights automatically. The document is publicly readable without subscribing.

If you want to present a design proposal, open it as a [design proposal PR](./design-proposals/README.md) first and link it from the agenda. That way attendees can read it before the call rather than during it.

## Meeting etiquette

- Add your name to the *Attendees* list for the day.
- Add any topic you want to discuss to the *Agenda* for the day.
- Flag your comments with `[name]` to make them easy to reference later.
- Meetings are recorded and published; if you would rather not appear in the
recording, say so at the start and keep your camera off.

## After the meeting

The host publishes the recording to the [community meetings playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLEIgpkcPkMHaXqndo8iMMLS64p4sBkHPg) and fills in the `Recording:` field for that date in the shared document. [meetings/README.md](./meetings/README.md) is then updated with the new row.
